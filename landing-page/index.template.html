Wear and care

Will sterling silver tarnish?

Yes, slowly. Sterling reacts with sulfur in the air and turns darker over time. Wearing it often actually slows this down, and a silver polishing cloth removes it.

Can I shower or swim in it?

It will not be ruined, but chlorine and salt water speed up tarnish. Take it off for the pool and the beach, and dry it after a shower.

How should I store it?

Lay it flat in a closed pouch or box, away from humidity. Keeping it separate from other jewelry stops the links from getting scratched.

What does the 925 stamp mean?

It means the metal is 92.5% pure silver, alloyed with copper for strength. That is the legal standard for sterling silver.
